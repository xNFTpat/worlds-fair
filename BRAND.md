# Still

Clarity before capital.

Still helps people understand a Solana liquidity pool before trying a paper allocation. The user selected the name on 5 October 2026. World's Fair is the repository and hackathon context; Still is the product name.

The temporary SVG mark combines a range bracket with two quiet horizontal bars. It is original editable vector artwork in `public/brand/`, intended as a working product mark. Final logo refinement and submission upload are deferred. Naming research is not trademark clearance.

## Interface rules

- Three main destinations: Pools, Paper portfolio, Strategies.
- One clear next step per pool: Open pre-flight. Suggested ranges appear only when the supported bin count and current price evidence permit them.
- State Pass, Caution and Fail in words as well as colour. Missing evidence stays visible and cannot become a Pass.
- Use Why? and Advanced disclosures for supporting detail. Keep dates, costs and downside accessible.
- Label paper actions. Optional devnet signing has its own review; paper features do not require a wallet.
- Use brief entry and hover motion. Numbers remain stable. Respect reduced motion throughout.
- Aim for 44px controls, readable contrast, visible keyboard focus and no horizontal overflow on mobile.

Implementation uses original CSS and SVG, without copied component-library code or a new motion dependency. No owner avatar or personal identity is part of the public brand.
